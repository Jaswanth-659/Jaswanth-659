# 💫 About Me:
I am a first-year Electronics and Communication Engineering student at PES University, based in Whitefield, Bengaluru. Driven by a first-principles builder ethos, I engineer at the intersection of full-stack web architectures, privacy-first local AI runtimes, and embedded hardware systems. From scaling live platforms like wornout.in and automating B2B pipelines with TestLoom to competing in high-speed hackathons, I focus on turning complex technical challenges into precise, functional realities.
